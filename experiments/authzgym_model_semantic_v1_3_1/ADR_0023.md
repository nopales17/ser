## ADR-0023 -- Authorize one frozen inexpensive-model semantic condition on AuthzGym v1.3

- Status: accepted
- Date: <acceptance date>
- Context: The controlled chain now supports four things and only four. V1.3
  source-local semantics are answerable under the bounded fixture grammar
  (development answerability pass, 56 cases, 2576 labels, certificate
  `d308e68b...`). Perfect v1.3 semantics can drive a frozen downstream
  component to useful inspection rankings: `est-repair-v1.3.1-B-1` (component
  class SHA-256 `88b77c5f343117b354b5039d571bf03bf8867fcd7fb4f8fc91c1e1aefca7a048`,
  module SHA-256 `f9c92317abc562ac5175f90074238c666de55d557b02e0de368841676f4d910d`)
  attains development canonical top-1 `0.75` / top-2 `1.0` / regret `0.116667`
  under gold semantics. B-1 then passed one-shot untouched successor
  confirmation on `confirmation_v1_3_1` (layouts 42/43, sealed
  `463d208f...`): top-1 `0.875`, top-2 `1.0`, regret `0.058333`, zero illegal
  targets, own-selection equivariance `40/40`, section-14 equivalence `40/40`,
  zero provider calls. Two things remain untested: **actual model production of
  the v1.3 semantic state**, and closed-loop routing. The next experiment
  changes exactly one link in the chain, `gold semantic response -> actual
  model semantic response`, and leaves everything downstream frozen. The
  accepted external research design (Astra) plus the accepted
  choice-set-primary clarification are translated normatively in
  `experiments/authzgym_model_semantic_v1_3_1/PREREGISTRATION.md`.
- Decision: Accept that preregistration as the governing specification for
  condition `model-semantic-v1.3.1-N1` and authorize, in the order it fixes:

  **D1. One model condition, frozen before any call.** Exactly one inexpensive
  configuration on the repository's previously exercised Nano-tier route,
  `patchersniper_praneeth/gpt-5.4-nano`, frozen as a complete request
  configuration after the zero-inference verification of section 9 of the
  preregistration. No model comparison, no automatic escalation, no prompt
  search, no self-critique, no voting, no examples selected from confirmation,
  and no semantic-response repair.

  **D2. The primary endpoint is choice-set preservation.** For canonical source
  `i`, `G_i` is the set of legal targets tied for maximum B-1 value under
  independently certified gold semantics and `M_i` the same set under the
  actual model response. The decision is preserved when `M_i` is non-empty and
  `M_i` is a subset of `G_i`. Evaluator-selected-target agreement is **not** the
  primary endpoint, because that selection uses the evaluator-only canonical
  ordinal to break B-1 ties. The frozen canonical ordinal remains
  evaluator-only, is used only where the existing usefulness scorer requires it,
  and never becomes model or controller input or part of the scientific
  definition of primary decision preservation. Exact argmax-set equality,
  evaluator-canonical selected-target agreement, top-two-set agreement, and tie
  dependence are reported separately and are never primary.

  **D3. The downstream-compatibility prerequisite, stated exactly.** AuthzGym
  v1.3 preregistration section 16 was never satisfied, and this decision does
  **not** claim that it was; no historical v1.3 freeze artifact is retroactively
  created and no historical report is modified to make it appear satisfied.
  ADR-0019 and the v1.3 preregistration remain the semantic authority for this
  condition, unchanged and unreinterpreted. The historical estimator's failed
  fresh-confirmation result (canonical top-2 `0.750` against `>= 0.80`) is
  **permanently preserved**, is never rescored, re-aggregated, averaged or
  superseded, and that component is not used here. What governs this condition
  instead is the separately versioned, sealed `est-repair-v1.3.1-B-1` successor
  confirmation (`experiments/authzgym_confirmation_v1_3_1/`, one-shot oracle
  `pass`, seal `463d208f...`), which is the applicable downstream-compatibility
  prerequisite, is already met, is not re-run, and is not extended to cover
  anything it did not measure. The spent `confirmation_v1_3` population stays
  unopened and the sealed `confirmation_v1_3_1` population is not reused as a
  model population.

  **D4. The condition's freeze artifacts are new and local.** The condition's
  own freeze artifacts and manifest --
  `FROZEN_INPUTS_MODEL_V1_3_1.json`, `FREEZE_CHECKLIST.md`,
  `MODEL_CONDITION.json`, `COST_GATE.json`, `RESTATED_HASHES.json` -- are
  created in `experiments/authzgym_model_semantic_v1_3_1/` and reference every
  inherited v1.3 and v1.3.1 input by hash.
  `experiments/authzgym_semantic_contract_v1_3/` remains protected and
  byte-unchanged, including its `MODEL_TRANSPORT_CONFIG.json`, whose
  `model_identifier` stays `unassigned_until_separate_inference_authorization`;
  this ADR is that separate authorization and assigns the identifier in the
  condition's own `MODEL_CONDITION.json`, inheriting that file's frozen limits
  and retry policy by hash reference. Nothing in this condition requires
  retroactively creating a historical v1.3 freeze artifact.

  **D5. Development.** The existing eight v1.3 development sources and seven
  variants under the frozen two-repeat schedule: 56 cases, 112 logical calls.
  No prompt or model tuning after outputs are observed. Development determines
  eligibility for confirmation and nothing else. `longest_artifact` is retained
  only as a separately reported diagnostic and never selects, gates or
  advances. Choice-set preservation at development requires `>= 7/8` canonical
  sources **separately on each repeat**; repeat agreement is reported
  separately and two Boolean preservation outcomes are never averaged into
  eligibility.

  **D6. Fresh confirmation, and only after a second authorization.** A new
  untouched model-semantic confirmation population is required: split
  `confirmation_model_v1_3_1`, proposed layouts `44/45`, admitted only if
  content-blind provenance establishes they were never generated or inspected;
  prospective collision-only fallback `46/47` then `48/49`; unchanged
  generator, unchanged converter, four families, seven variants, eight
  independent source instances, 56 cases, one call each. If all prospectively
  listed pairs fail the preregistered content-blind eligibility and collision
  conditions, execution stops and new authority is requested; the sequence is
  never extended automatically. Confirmation is not generated during
  development. Generation, access and inference all require a separate
  post-development authorization naming the frozen implementation hash, the
  development-report hash, the model-configuration hash and the manifest hash.

  **D7. Gates.** Primary: choice-set preservation `>= 7/8`. Mandatory supporting
  gates are those accepted by Astra, enumerated with their exact denominators in
  preregistration section 8. Where a threshold is frozen in existing repository
  authority it is reused unchanged. The three thresholds the accepted research
  design supplies are incorporated as given: paired degradation -- top-1 decline
  `<= 0.125`, top-2 decline `<= 0.125`, mean positive excess regret `<= 0.05`;
  model-conditioned own-selection equivariance -- `80/80` repeat-matched
  development transformation comparisons and `40/40` confirmation comparisons;
  directional-effect continuity -- precision `>= 0.60` and recall `>= 0.50`.
  **Every one of these is a prospective engineering advancement screen over
  eight source instances; none is a population estimate and none may be reported
  as one.** Candidate effects remain deterministic consequences of facts and are
  never double-counted as independent semantic-capability evidence.

  **D7a. Effect self-consistency (S-7) is adjudicated `diagnostic_only`.** It
  remains a mandatory measured and reported semantic/interface diagnostic, but
  has **no independent advancement threshold and no veto power** over
  development eligibility or untouched-confirmation success. It is computed
  exactly from the model's submitted facts and submitted effects using the
  frozen public v1.3 effect truth table and the public candidate family;
  response-level and field-level consistency are both reported, every violation
  is enumerated, and invalid or missing responses are kept separate and never
  imputed consistent. No response repair, no semantic retry, and no exclusion
  because of inconsistency. B-1 receives the model's **actual submitted effect
  values**. Diagnostic substitutions never satisfy a gate, and the `D0`/`D3`
  analysis is retained to localize the contribution of inconsistencies. The
  directional-effect-versus-gold gate (S-9) and the directional-effect
  continuity gate (S-15) are unchanged. An S-7 violation remains a semantic
  error, but **by itself** it cannot produce `semantic_screen_below_threshold`,
  suppress the primary or downstream result, block `development_eligible`, or
  block `bounded_model_semantic_compatibility_confirmed`. Historical
  specifications and results remain unchanged. The exact metric, reporting
  scopes and structural non-gating guarantees are preregistration sections 8.6
  and 12.1.

  **D8. Error propagation.** The frozen analysis chain -- model semantic error
  -> effect consequence -> B-1 salient relation/category state -> target
  values/tie groups -> choice set -> usefulness/regret -- is operationalized in
  preregistration section 10 with five mutually exclusive, exhaustive,
  precedence-ordered classes: `malformed_or_missing`,
  `structurally_catastrophic`, `decision_changing`, `value_harmless`,
  `decision_harmless`. Evaluator-only diagnostic substitutions are computed
  without ever repairing, replacing or rewriting the actual measured response.

  **D9. Calls, retries, spend.** Development 112 logical calls; confirmation 56
  logical calls, only after a development pass and the later authorization. At
  most one identical-byte transport replay and one structural-response retry per
  logical call. **No retry for valid-but-semantically-wrong responses.** Every
  attempt is kept. Hidden SDK/client retries are disabled. Total hard spend
  ceiling `$2.50` covering the entire model condition: development,
  confirmation, all permitted retries, failed submissions and ambiguous
  transport submissions. If the verified route tariff cannot support the frozen
  schedule under that ceiling, execution stops before inference; the schedule is
  never reduced. No automatic model escalation after failure.

  **D10. Repository and governance corrections, prospective only.** After -- and
  only after -- this decision is accepted, reconcile the living
  `state/STATUS.yaml`, `plan/ROADMAP.md`, `MAP.md`, `experiments/README.md` and
  the regenerated `state/CONTEXT_PACKET.md` with the already-completed, sealed,
  passing B-1 successor confirmation. For new artifacts, **raw-file SHA-256 is
  the authoritative frozen-artifact byte hash**; where a canonicalized JSON
  digest is also useful it is recorded separately and explicitly as
  `canonical_json_sha256`, and **multiple digest types are never called simply
  `population_hash`**. Require file-open-level access logging for this condition.
  Historical ledgers are not rewritten and historical seals are not recomputed
  under the new convention.

- Why: Every link in this chain except one has been exercised under a frozen
  protocol, and the one unexercised link is the only one the project's central
  question depends on. Changing exactly that link, against an instrument whose
  answerability and firewall already pass and a downstream component that has
  already passed an untouched confirmation, is the smallest experiment that can
  produce information about it. Making choice-set preservation primary removes
  the evaluator-only ordinal from the scientific definition of success, so the
  endpoint measures what the model's semantic state does to the decision rather
  than what a private tie-break does to the measurement. Requiring a `subset`
  relation rather than equality lets a model state legitimately refine a gold
  tie without credit for introducing a target the gold state never considered
  maximal.
- Alternatives rejected: using evaluator-selected-target agreement as primary;
  exposing the canonical ordinal to the model or the component; running more
  than one model configuration, or escalating after a failure; searching prompts,
  adding self-critique, voting, or examples drawn from confirmation; retrying a
  structurally valid but semantically wrong response; repairing a measured
  response before scoring it; reusing, reopening, resampling or characterizing
  `confirmation_v1_3` or `confirmation_v1_3_1`; generating confirmation during
  development; extending the layout fallback sequence past `48/49`; reducing the
  frozen schedule to fit the spend ceiling; changing B-1, the adapter, any v1.3
  semantic rule, any threshold, the usefulness target, or the tie rule;
  inventing a new statistical null threshold for choice-set preservation;
  claiming or engineering a retroactive pass of v1.3 section 16; editing frozen
  v1.3 artifacts, or creating historical v1.3 freeze artifacts after the fact, to
  satisfy section 16; modifying any historical report; reinterpreting,
  superseding or averaging away the preserved fresh-confirmation failure of the
  historical estimator; reporting any of the section-D7 screens as a population
  estimate; giving effect self-consistency an advancement threshold, a veto, or
  any gating role at implementation level; excluding, repairing or retrying a
  response because it is effect-inconsistent; passing B-1 a truth-table-corrected
  effect vector in place of the model's submitted one; rewriting
  historical seals to match the new hashing convention; and adding closed-loop
  routing, Jev, model escalation, representation intervention, or richer SER
  architecture to this condition.
- Consequences: Condition `model-semantic-v1.3.1-N1` becomes an authorized
  model-semantic study whose artifacts live under
  `experiments/authzgym_model_semantic_v1_3_1/`. A pass establishes only bounded
  compatibility of `source artifact -> this model/configuration -> v1.3
  semantics -> frozen B-1` inside this controlled generator. It does **not**
  establish authorization competence, general code understanding, closed-loop
  routing, adaptive-routing advantage, SER architecture superiority, GitLab
  transfer, or real-world action value, and it promotes no concept. A clean
  untouched-confirmation pass makes closed-loop **experimental design** the next
  research task; it does not authorize closed-loop execution, and it does not
  license any assumption that `longest_artifact` is adequate -- the recorded
  read-outs (development top-1 `0.125`/top-2 `0.375` for the historical
  estimator; `0.25`/`0.25` for B-1 on the sealed confirmation) warn explicitly
  against that. Phase 5 remains active. Phase 5B remains blocked until this
  condition produces a valid outcome. Historical v1.2, v1.3, est-repair and
  sealed-confirmation artifacts, responses, reports, hashes, classifiers and
  blockers remain immutable.
- Preserved under this decision: `src/ser/authzgym/policies.py` (SHA-256
  `092a7a87...`), `src/ser/evaluation/authz_v1_3.py` (SHA-256 `60b1cb5d...`),
  `src/ser/authzgym/policies_v1_3_1.py` (SHA-256 `f9c92317...`),
  `src/ser/authzgym/generation.py` (SHA-256 `03bfe556...`),
  `src/ser/authzgym/v1_3_population.py` (SHA-256 `ac4b8d47...`), every AuthzGym
  v1.3 semantic rule, prompt, schema, population, threshold, usefulness target,
  certificate and validation result, every `est-repair-v1.3.1` artifact, and the
  entire sealed `experiments/authzgym_confirmation_v1_3_1/` directory. The spent
  `confirmation_v1_3` population is not opened, counted, sampled, hashed afresh
  or characterized. `confirmation_v1_3_1` is likewise not reused as a model
  population and is not reopened for any purpose in this condition.
- Revisit when: The development schedule terminates with a preregistered
  outcome label and a frozen development report; or the model-condition
  verification, the cost gate, the gold-adequacy gate, the answerability gate,
  the firewall gate or the freeze checklist fires a blocker; or an
  implementation contradiction appears that this decision and the
  preregistration do not resolve. Confirmation generation requires its own
  separate decision naming the four hashes in D6. Closed-loop routing requires
  its own decision, its own preregistration, and the separate evidence listed in
  preregistration section 13.
