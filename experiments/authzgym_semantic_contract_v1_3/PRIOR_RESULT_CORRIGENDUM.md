# AuthzGym v1.2 prior-result corrigendum

Status: accepted living-document correction under ADR-0019

Date: 2026-09-18

This corrigendum supplements, and does not rewrite, the frozen v1.2,
transport-envelope, stronger-model, or semantic-bottleneck artifacts. Their raw
responses, hashes, reports, classifiers, and contemporaneous interpretations
remain historical records.

## Claims that remain valid in their recorded scope

- The original v1.2 run's transport failure counts, provider-attempt counts,
  response bytes, parse/schema outcomes, resource accounting, hashes, and
  `contract_unstable` classification remain valid implementation observations.
- The transport-envelope study obtained 128/128 provider responses with the
  recorded tunnel, retry, cleanup, token, cost, and response-schema behavior.
  Its `transport_stable` result remains valid for that exact development
  transport schedule.
- All 128 transport-envelope responses were first-attempt valid under the frozen
  v1.2 JSON schema. This remains evidence of wire-format/response-contract
  reliability, not semantic correctness.
- The stronger-model smoke and 16 executed development calls had the recorded
  transport and schema validity, costs, responses, and preregistered stop event.
  Confirmation received zero provider/model calls.
- Mini's observed output instability across the examined equivalent public
  presentations remains a descriptive observation.
- The independent syntax-level finding that Mini omitted relations for 64 of 65
  visibly called legal uninspected targets remains a descriptive observation of
  those responses. It does not certify every old relation gold label.
- The offline audit correctly established concrete v1.2 benchmark defects and
  correctly stopped additional inference at zero incremental cost.

## Claims that are now descriptive or conditional only

- Every v1.2 aggregate fact, candidate-effect, unresolved-relation, semantic
  signal, and model-conditioned downstream score is conditional on the frozen
  v1.2 task/scorer and may not be treated as a clean estimate of the published
  source-direct task.
- The stronger-model 16/32 futility calculation remains a correct calculation
  relative to the frozen v1.2 denominators and thresholds. It is not a v1.3
  bound and does not establish that the model would fail an answerable task.
- Nano and Mini oracle top-1/top-2/regret results show only that the unchanged
  estimator reproduced authored usefulness when supplied curated evaluator
  observations. They do not validate v1.2 answerability, the local-cue semantics
  adopted for v1.3, or real epistemic action value.
- Static v1.1 remains a deterministic construction/calibration record, not
  blanket certification of the later semantic target.
- The localization's effect-answerability counts are conditional because its
  predicate reused the evaluator's unpublished effect mapping. Its old relation
  check likewise reused shared helper semantics. The concrete counterexamples
  still justify the stop, but those helpers are not independent certification of
  a replacement gold standard.

## Capability interpretations withdrawn

- The repository must not describe Nano or Mini as having established a clean
  v1.2 fact/effect capability floor.
- V1.2 effect scores are not a second independent semantic-capability signal;
  the labels were derived through an unpublished evaluator rule.
- The stronger-model result does not establish model inability, representation
  insufficiency, benchmark-wide relation competence, or broad authorization
  competence.
- No v1.2 result supports or rejects H-001, H-016, H-017, or H-018; no
  architecture comparison or E-* finding is admitted.
- V1.2 scores must not be compared numerically with future v1.3 scores as model
  improvement or regression. Any rescoring of stored responses is an explicitly
  labeled offline diagnostic limited to propositions with demonstrably unchanged
  meanings and is not v1.3 performance.

## Privileged-summary correction

The v1.2 `maximal_public_summary` implementation populated normal model-visible
`facts_seen` and `candidate_support` from `oracle_content`. The downstream
analysis also reconstructed prior normal state through the oracle helper. That
flow conflicts with the declared ordinary-condition firewall and the v1.2
statement that oracle content never enters prompts or normal controller state.

Consequently:

- the 16 successful Nano transport-study calls using a non-empty maximal summary
  were privileged-input exposures and cannot represent the declared normal
  condition;
- normal-condition semantic and downstream conclusions from those calls are
  withdrawn;
- the executed Mini prefix was not exposed through this particular path because
  it contained no maximal-summary case, although its independent answerability
  defects still block clean capability attribution; and
- the historical `contract_stable` label is retained only as a statement about
  exact transport and wire/schema reliability. It must not be read as a claim
  that the complete ordinary semantic condition was information-boundary-valid
  or answerable.

No historical artifact is deleted or edited because of this correction.
