# Open questions

The canonical entries are `Q-001` through `Q-009` in
`theory/IDEA_MAP.yaml`. Current high-value questions are:

- `Q-001`: which policy objective should combine decision relevance and
  information value once raw vector costs, latency, risk, and budget constraints
  are preserved without universal scalarization;
- `Q-002`: which useful structure should be added beyond the accepted minimum
  epistemic-state entitlement, update, identity, and provenance invariants;
- `Q-003`: whether repeated implementations eventually justify a common content
  envelope, after Phase 2 rejected a universal EpistemicUnit from the core;
- `Q-004`: Phase 1 found no unchanged reuse and recorded generalization patterns,
  deferred environment assets, and excluded prior solution logic; any actual
  future transfer remains an explicit decision;
- `Q-005`: what a compressor may discard without damaging future decisions;
- `Q-006`: whether any of the nine deferred coupling names has irreducible
  semantics beyond ordinary policy or updater logic;
- `Q-007`: which policy stopping rule minimizes submission/abstention regret
  under vector cost after Phase 2 made STOP first-class;
- `Q-008`: whether SER and SERT are the right names;
- `Q-009`: what cross-environment evidence, matched baselines, holdouts,
  ablations, uncertainty analyses, and independent confirmation warrant
  scientific promotion.

Questions remain in the cold map even after resolution. Resolution updates their
status and notes, links the evidence or ADR, and preserves the prior conceptual
history.
